# Awesome Reading Lists [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> Book reading lists from interesting people around the web, plus curated lists maintained in this repo.

A reading list is one of the highest-signal ways to find your next great book: instead of an algorithm, you get the actual bookshelf of a person whose thinking you admire.

**Prefer a browsable UI?** Check out [readsfrom ↗](https://readsfrom.kapps.dev) — a companion web app by this repo's maintainer that collects book recommendations from public posts, with the quote and the source link always attached.

## Personal Bookshelves

### Founders and Investors

- [Bill Gates ↗](https://www.gatesnotes.com/Books) - Reviews of everything he reads, plus his famous yearly summer and holiday lists.
- [Patrick Collison ↗](https://patrickcollison.com/bookshelf) - The Stripe cofounder's bookshelf, with his favorites marked.
- [Warren Buffett](lists/warren_buffett.md) - Curated in this repo from his shareholder letters and interviews.

### Engineers and Technologists

- [Alexey Migutsky ↗](https://github.com/mr-mig/booklist) - A "mind-expanding" booklist for engineers, maintained as a GitHub repo.
- [Dan Luu ↗](https://danluu.com/programming-books/) - Programming book recommendations with unusually thoughtful commentary.
- [Jeff Atwood ↗](https://blog.codinghorror.com/recommended-reading-for-developers/) - The Coding Horror recommended reading list for developers.
- [Pamela Fox ↗](http://www.pamelafox.org/readinglist) - A long-running personal reading log.
- [Radek Pietruszewski ↗](http://radex.io/books) - Books with short, honest reviews.
- [Rakhim Davletkaliyev ↗](https://rakhim.org/bookshelf) - A bookshelf with ratings and notes.

### Writers and Thinkers

- [Alexey Guzey ↗](https://guzey.com/books/) - An opinionated list of the books he found most valuable.
- [Blas Moros ↗](https://blas.com/books/) - Hundreds of in-depth book summaries (The Rabbit Hole).
- [Cosma Shalizi ↗](https://bactra.org/reviews/) - Decades of erudite reviews across science, statistics and history (The Bactra Review).
- [Derek Sivers ↗](https://sive.rs/book) - Every book he's read since 2007, rated 1-10 with detailed notes.
- [Gwern Branwen ↗](https://gwern.net/review/book) - Over a thousand book reviews with ratings.
- [James Clear ↗](https://jamesclear.com/book-recommendations) - Recommended reading organized by category, with summaries.
- [Jason Evanish ↗](https://jasonevanish.com/books) - Books for managers, leaders and startup people.
- [Nat Eliason ↗](https://www.nateliason.com/notes) - Detailed notes and ratings for hundreds of books.
- [Ryan Holiday ↗](https://ryanholiday.net/reading-list/) - Archive of his monthly reading-recommendation newsletter.
- [Scott Alexander ↗](https://slatestarcodex.com/tag/book-review/) - Deep, essay-length book reviews from Slate Star Codex.
- [Shane Parrish ↗](https://fs.blog/reading) - Farnam Street's reading guides and yearly "what I read" lists.
- [Tim Ferriss ↗](https://tim.blog/2017/11/18/booklist/) - The books most recommended by guests across Tools of Titans and Tribe of Mentors.

## Community and Topic Lists

## Discovery Sites

- [Five Books ↗](https://fivebooks.com/) - Experts pick and discuss the five best books in their field, in interview form.
- [Goodbooks ↗](https://www.goodbooks.io/) - Thousands of book recommendations from well-known people.
- [Most Recommended Books ↗](https://www.mostrecommendedbooks.com/) - Aggregates book recommendations from famous founders, investors and authors.
- [Read This Twice ↗](https://www.readthistwice.com/) - Search any public figure and see the books they've recommended, with sources.
- [readsfrom ↗](https://readsfrom.kapps.dev) - Book recommendations traced back to the public post where they were made.
- [The Greatest Books ↗](https://thegreatestbooks.org/) - A meta-list generated from hundreds of "best books" lists.
- [Wonderhum ↗](https://wonderhum.com/books/) - Books mentioned on 60+ podcasts and shows, each mention quoted word for word with its timestamp.

## Publishers

- [Stripe Press ↗](https://press.stripe.com/) - Beautifully produced editions of "ideas for progress" — a reading list in publisher form.

## Contributing

Contributions are welcome! Found a great reading list, or a broken link? Please read the [contribution guidelines](CONTRIBUTING.md) and open a pull request.

Links are checked automatically every week, so the list stays rot-free.
